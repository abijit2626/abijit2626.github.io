03 / RULES

Four rules I keep rediscovering

Hover (or tap) a card to flip it.

01
RULE

Capability is not intent.

WHYTrusted software installs keyboard hooks, opens raw sockets and reads the clipboard. Flagging capability alone builds a tool analysts learn to ignore.
02
RULE

Persistence amplifies risk.

WHYA single observation is a data point. Behaviour that survives over time is a signal.
03
RULE

Every signal explains itself.

WHYIf I can't say why it scored what it did, nobody can triage it. A model score never crosses a threshold on its own.
04
RULE

Correlation beats volume.

WHYOne high-confidence incident from a complete attack chain is worth more than a thousand isolated alerts.
