    03 / RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

    Four rules I keep arriving at independently.

    Across unrelated projects, the same ideas keep coming back.

    01
    Capability is not intent.

    Trusted software installs keyboard hooks, opens raw sockets and reads the clipboard. Flagging capability alone builds a tool analysts learn to ignore.

    02
    Persistence amplifies risk.

    A single observation is a data point. Behaviour that survives over time is a signal.

    03
    Every signal explains itself.

    If I can’t say why something scored what it did, nobody can triage it. A model score never crosses a threshold on its own.

    04
    Correlation beats volume.

    One high-confidence incident from a complete attack chain is worth more than a thousand isolated alerts.
